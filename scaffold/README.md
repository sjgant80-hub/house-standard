<details><summary>How it is built</summary>

Built from [the house standard](https://github.com/sjgant80-hub/house-standard): a crystal hero, receipts, a stack,
a glass that runs on your device, and limits one click away. Claims are registered in [CLAIMS.md](CLAIMS.md) before
the run, a seal freezes everything that can change a result, every kernel is mutation-gated, and the page re-derives
every number in your browser.
